\paragraph*{What differs inside the pronoun-free pairs.}
\vekalet{The run on pairs with no pronoun contrast (\S\ref{sec:dial}) used $37{,}426$ pairs from 7 source datasets. The table compares the chosen with the rejected side of each pair. The second person is equal in $99.96\%$ of pairs and absent from most, but the first person is not: the chosen side has $0.91$ fewer first-person forms per 1{,}000 on average, 53\% of pairs are equal and 22\% have more on the chosen side. The chosen side is longer by a median of $11$ tokens, while sentence count, list lines and code fences differ by a median of zero. Intervals resample the source datasets 1{,}000 times; with seven sources they are coarse. The reading is descriptive, and its path was committed before the count.}
