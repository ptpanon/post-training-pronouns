The re-scoring of \S\ref{sec:dial} draws one response per prompt from the T\"ulu-3 preference mixture, $3{,}000$ in all from $87{,}311$ scanned, keeping only responses that accept a person swap. Ten rules replace second-person marking with an impersonal form in a fixed order, checked before scoring for subject--verb agreement and sentence-initial capitals; the reverse direction uses the nine that invert, since \emph{your}$\rightarrow$\emph{the} does not. The median rewrite changes $0.85\%$ of the characters ($0.15$ to $4.2\%$ between the 5th and 95th percentiles); a typical pair turns \emph{``is not an object you can ride on''} into \emph{``is not an object it is possible to ride on''}. Each response and its rewrite are scored by ArmoRM-Llama3-8B-v0.1 and Skywork-Reward-Llama-3.1-8B, and the statistic is the paired difference, person-free minus person-marked, with a prompt-clustered 95\% interval over $1{,}000$ resamples. The placebo shuffles words that carry no person marking within the same response until as many characters have moved as the swap moved, so it moves as much text without touching the persons --- though, unlike the rewrite, it breaks the syntax. The rewrite moves ArmoRM by $-0.0004$ $[-0.00068,-0.00012]$ against $-0.011$ $[-0.0115,-0.0105]$ for its placebo, and Skywork by $-0.597$ $[-0.68,-0.50]$ against $-5.29$ $[-5.56,-5.02]$, pooled over both directions: $0.006$ to $0.031$ standard deviations of the score, with the placebo moving $8.9$ to $27$ times further. The pool mixes two directions, $1{,}765$ pairs that remove the persons from a person-marked original and $1{,}235$ that add them to a person-free one. Read as the rewritten version minus the original, removing them moves ArmoRM by $-0.0016$ $[-0.00202,-0.00105]$ and Skywork by $-1.52$ $[-1.63,-1.41]$, and adding them moves ArmoRM by $-0.0013$ $[-0.00144,-0.00105]$ and Skywork by $-0.73$ $[-0.84,-0.63]$. Both scorers rate whichever version was rewritten lower, whichever way the persons move, so the pooled sign follows the mix of directions and the test reads the edit rather than the person marking; this split was read after the fact and is descriptive. Both also move far more under the equal-length shuffle, which is no like-for-like yardstick because it damages the sentence the rewrite keeps grammatical. A power check on the first $300$ pairs cleared the detectable-effect bar on both scorers before the full run. \textbf{This test changes both the scorer and the corpus of the association it answers}: that association is read in UltraFeedback's ratings, which its dataset card says GPT-4 assigned, and the rewrite in the T\"ulu-3 mixture. No model card of the sixteen models names either reward model, which does not exclude one inside a pipeline that is not disclosed, and Skywork-Reward-Llama-3.1-8B names Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, one of the panel's own aligned checkpoints, as its base model: a scorer built on the panel's own lineage does not penalise the persons either.
