\paragraph*{Past the first 160 tokens.}
\vekalet{On the debate prompts most aligned outputs reach the 160-token cap, so their rates describe openings. The instruction prompts of Appendix~\ref{app:D4} were generated in a 2{,}048-token window, and there we read the first 160 tokens of each output and the rest as separate texts (aligned checkpoint in its own template, base checkpoint in the in-context assistant prompt, the B1 cell of Table~\ref{tab:cells}). In the first 160 tokens ``you'' falls in 16 of 16 models, all 16 clearing the paired placebo, and ``I'' in 12 (2 rise). After them, Mistral-7B-v0.3 and the three Gemma-3 models write too little to read ($0$ to $22$ pairs). Of the other 12, ``you'' falls with an interval excluding zero in 6 and rises in none, at a median shift of $-8.85$ per 1{,}000 against $-8.89$ in the first 160 tokens, but on a median of $230$ pairs rather than $1{,}195$, and only one model clears its placebo. ``I'' falls in 1 of 12 after the first 160 tokens, at a median of $-0.35$ against $-2.53$. The fall in ``you'' keeps its size where later text exists, then, but the later text is too sparse to separate it model by model, and the first-person fall is concentrated in the openings on these prompts. The reading is descriptive; its path was committed before the count.}
