\paragraph*{Qwen2.5 bases read through their own chat template.}
\vekalet{Qwen2.5's base checkpoints ship a chat template of their own, so the chat-template contrast can also be read with each checkpoint in its own template and the base in distribution. The two templates render our prompts identically except for the default system line (``You are a helpful assistant.'' against ``You are Qwen, created by Alibaba Cloud. You are a helpful assistant.''). We read four of the six sizes (1.5B, 3B, 7B and 14B); the 32B generations exist and were not read, and no 72B base generations were made. ``I'' falls in all four ($-10.36$, $-5.89$, $-8.81$, $-9.35$ per 1,000 tokens), every interval excluding zero. ``You'' falls in 3B and 7B ($-4.23$ and $-6.38$), is unresolved in 14B ($-0.64$, interval covering zero) and rises in 1.5B ($+3.04$), where the degeneracy filter keeps $2{,}594$ of $6{,}528$ pairs against $6{,}236$ to $6{,}373$ in the other three. With the base in distribution the pattern is the one \S\ref{sec:deperson} reports under the aligned template: ``I'' falls and ``you'' splits. The reading is descriptive and was registered before it was computed.}
