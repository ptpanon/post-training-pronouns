On $300$ ordinary instructions, each checkpoint in its own format, ``you'' falls in all 16 and ``I'' in $12$, and the aligned range of ``you'' is $2.3$-fold. With the base on a person-stripped in-context prompt, ``you'' again falls in all 16 and ``I'' in $12$. On an identical string the aligned model reads a format it was not trained on and the sign of ``you'' splits ($11$ down on the released in-context prompt; $6$ down, $7$ up on the person-stripped one) while ``I'' falls in $9$ and $10$ (Appendix~\ref{app:D4})
