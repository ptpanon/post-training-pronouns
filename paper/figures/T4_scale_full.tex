\paragraph*{Scale sequences behind the supervised stage's share.}
\vekalet{\textbf{The supervised stage's share does not follow scale.} Within OLMo-2 --- one pipeline read at three scales --- the supervised stage carries $31\%$, $11\%$, $45\%$ of the end-to-end withdrawal at 7B, 13B, 32B, which is not monotone in scale, while the withdrawal itself does fall monotonically ($10.5$, $6.3$, $4.8$ per thousand). T\"ulu-3 gives two points in the same direction of scale and the share rises rather than falls: $19\%$ at 8B against $27\%$ at 70B. \textbf{Size moves the amount withdrawn; \emph{within a pipeline} it never makes the supervised stage the larger one.}}
