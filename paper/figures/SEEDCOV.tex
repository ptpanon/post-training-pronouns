\paragraph{Seed coverage of the register battery.}
\vekalet{The sixteen-model panel was generated with a single sampling seed per checkpoint, and the generation manifests of that panel do not record which seed. The single-seed status is an inference from the pipeline default rather than a logged fact. A separate multi-seed audit regenerates each model with four sampling seeds and re-reads it with the same estimator. It has completed \textbf{all 16} (the manifest lists them), and they include the model that carries the smallest effect in the panel, which is the one a seed artifact would break first. Re-read with four generation seeds, all of them hold: the shift stays negative, the prompt-clustered interval still excludes zero, and the effect still exceeds the paired placebo. Four-seed pooled estimates differ from the single-seed ones by $0.05$--$0.61$ per thousand, against seed-to-seed spreads of $0.08$--$0.52$. What survives the re-read is the sign, the interval and the placebo, in all 16; the \emph{magnitude} does not travel as well. 8 of the 16 single-seed readings fall outside their own four-seed range (up to 5.3 seed-sd, Gemma-3-4B, and 4.7, OLMo-2-32B), so we read magnitudes at the four-seed pooled value wherever it exists and mark the rest. }
