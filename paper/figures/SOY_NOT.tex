$H$ and $\eta^2$ are sign-free: under the null they are positive, so each is read against its own permutation centre (5.0 and $33\%$ here), not against zero. \textbf{The two are not comparable across factors either}: lineage is a six-level factor on sixteen points with two singleton groups, and a six-level null already accounts for $33\%$ of the variance where a binary property's null centre is $7\%$, so the share is read against its own null and never against another factor's share.
